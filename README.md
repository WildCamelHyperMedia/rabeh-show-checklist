# RABEH — Weekly Show Checklist

The producer's checklist for the weekly RABEH live show, by Wild Camel HyperMedia, Dubai.

**Open the checklist:** https://wildcamelhypermedia.github.io/rabeh-show-checklist/

## How to use it

- **Open the page on show day.** It opens on this week's show (Friday). Use the arrows or the date to open another show.
- **Tick each item as it is done.** The time is recorded automatically at the moment you tick. Tap the time to correct it.
- **Crew arrival.** Tick *Present* when someone arrives — the arrival time is filled in for you, and you can change it.
- **Times with a NOW button** (mic fitted, on set, recording confirmed, post-show) take the current time with one tap.
- **Notes and issues** are saved as you type.
- Everything is saved on the device straight away and sent to the production Google Sheet as soon as there is a connection. The pill at the top shows the state: *Saved to sheet*, *Saving…*, *Offline · on device* (no network) or *Sheet not saving* (the network is fine, the Sheet is not answering — tap the pill to see why). In every case the changes are kept on the device and sent again automatically.
- **A stray tap** that unticks a row or replaces a time shows **Undo** at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds.
- **Print / Save PDF** at the bottom of the page gives a paper copy of the finished checklist.

## First time on a phone or laptop

1. **On an iPhone or iPad, do this first:** open the page in Safari, tap the share button and choose **Add to Home Screen**. Then always open the checklist from that icon. Safari itself forgets the access code, the offline copy and any changes not yet sent after a week without a visit; the Home Screen app does not. The Home Screen app keeps its own data, so connect (step 2) from the icon, not in Safari.
2. Tap **Connect** and enter the producers' access code.

On Android, "Add to Home screen" in the browser menu makes the page open like an app too.

If the show is finished on Friday night but the post-show checks are not, the page keeps opening on that show until they are ticked (or until Tuesday), so hand-over on Saturday goes into the right show. The big button under the date opens the next show.
